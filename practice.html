Diesel engine performance

Sheet 4

A 6-cylinder four-stroke diesel: bore 100 mm, stroke 120 mm, 2400 rpm. Brake torque 280 N·m, fuel 14 kg/h, CV 43 000 kJ/kg. Find BP, bmep, bsfc and brake thermal efficiency.

Show answer

Swept volume = 5.655 L; BP = 2πNT/60 = 70.4 kW; bmep = BP·2/(Vs·N/60) = 6.22 bar; bsfc = 14/70.4 = 0.199 kg/kWh; ηB = 70.4/(14/3600×43000) = 42.1 %.

3

Combined-cycle efficiency

Sheet 6

A gas turbine with η₁ = 0.32 rejects its exhaust to a steam plant of η₂ = 0.35; 20 % of the fuel heat is lost to the stack between them. Find the combined efficiency.

Show answer

η = η₁ + η₂(1 − η₁ − xL) = 0.32 + 0.35(1 − 0.32 − 0.20) = 48.8 %.

Merkel number

Counter-flow wet tower, water 40 → 30 °C, Twb = 22 °C, Tdb = 28 °C, L/G = 1.2. Find KaV/L (4-point).

Show answer

KaV/L ≈ 1.03. Method: ha1 at (28, 22), then four temperatures 31, 34, 36, 39 °C.

5

Dry cooling tower stacking

Finals 2024

A surface condenser at 45 °C is cooled by water that enters at 28 °C and leaves at 38 °C. State TTD and the minimum possible air temperature if the tower approach is 8 °C.

Show answer

TTD = 45 − 38 = 7 °C; cooling range = 10 °C; the tower must return water at 28 °C, so the air inlet is at most 28 − 8 = 20 °C dry bulb.

6

Back-pressure turbine

Sheet 6

Steam at 20 bar, 300 °C expands to 3 bar with η = 0.70 for process use. Find the specific work and the exhaust enthalpy.

Show answer

Work = h₁ − h₂ = 273.0 kJ/kg, with h₁(20 bar, 300 °C) = 3024.3 and the actual exhaust enthalpy h₂ = h₁ − 0.70(h₁ − h₂ₛ) = 2751.2 kJ/kg (not necessarily dry saturated). The exhaust heat is the process heat.
